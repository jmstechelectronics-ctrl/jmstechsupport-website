Privacy Policy

Last updated: 28 July 2026

JMS Tech Support ("we", "our", or "us") provides on-site IT support, Wi-Fi troubleshooting, computer repair and security camera services across the Clarence Valley. This policy explains how we handle information submitted through this website.

1. Information We Collect

When you submit a support or quote request, we collect the details you provide, including your name, email address, phone number, suburb, service required, preferred contact method and job details.

2. How We Use Information

We use this information only to respond to your request, provide support or a quote, arrange a visit where needed, and communicate about your job. We do not sell or rent your personal information.

3. Form Submissions

Website contact forms are delivered through FormSubmit to JMS Tech Support. Information from a form is used only to respond to that enquiry.

4. Cookies & Tracking

We use Google Analytics and the Meta Pixel to understand website use and measure the effectiveness of advertising. These services use cookies and similar technologies to collect usage and device information. Meta may process this information under its own privacy policy. You can disable cookies in your browser settings.

5. Data Storage & Access

Enquiry information is kept only for as long as reasonably necessary to provide support, quote for work, meet record-keeping requirements, or resolve a related issue. Access is limited to JMS Tech Support.

6. Your Rights

You can request access to, correction of, or deletion of information we hold about you, subject to any legal record-keeping obligations.
